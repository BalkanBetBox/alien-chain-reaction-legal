Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-05-15  ·  Last Updated: 2026-06-08

This Privacy Policy describes how Alien Chain Reaction (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile game on Android or iOS.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and refrain from using it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect a minimal amount of anonymous data to operate the App, fix bugs, and serve advertising. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or any other personally identifying information.

1.1 Gameplay analytics

When you play, the App sends anonymous event records to Google Firebase Analytics. Examples of events:

  • A level was started or completed
  • A continuation (revive) was offered, accepted, or declined
  • Aggregated session counts and screen views

These events do not contain any information that identifies you personally.

1.2 Crash diagnostics

If the App crashes or encounters an unrecoverable error, Google Firebase Crashlytics receives a crash report containing:

  • The error message and call stack
  • Device model, operating system version, and App version
  • An anonymous Firebase Installation ID, which lets us count unique affected devices but does not identify you

1.3 Advertising identifier

To show advertisements — primarily rewarded video (e.g. when you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an extra rocket), and occasional full-screen ads between games — we use Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), which accesses your device's Advertising ID (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS). This identifier:

  • Is set by your operating system, not by us
  • Can be reset or disabled by you at any time in your device settings
  • Is used by advertising networks to limit ad frequency and serve more relevant content

1.4 Automatically collected technical data

Firebase services and the Google Play Services framework collect standard device data such as: device type, OS version, language, country (derived from IP at request time, not stored as exact location), and app version. We use this data in aggregate form only.

2. How We Use the Information

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • Identify and fix crashes and bugs
  • Understand how players progress through levels so we can improve game balance
  • Serve and pay for the rewarded video advertisements that fund continued development
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or abuse

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how it handles data:

Service Provider Purpose Policy
Firebase Analytics Google LLC Anonymous gameplay analytics link
Firebase Crashlytics Google LLC Crash diagnostics link
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) Google LLC Rewarded and interstitial advertising link
Google Play Services Google LLC Platform support, advertising ID link

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it.

The App is rated for a general teen and adult audience. Parental controls on the operating system can be used to restrict access.

5. International Users — GDPR and CCPA

5.1 European Economic Area / United Kingdom (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK, the legal basis for processing your data is:

  • Legitimate interest for crash diagnostics and basic operational analytics
  • Consent for advertising personalization and non-essential analytics

Before any non-essential data is collected, the App shows a consent dialog. You can withdraw consent at any time through the in-app settings; doing so will disable personalized advertising and detailed analytics. You have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 8.

5.2 California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what categories of personal information have been collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the “sale” of personal information. We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense; however, the sharing of advertising identifiers with advertising partners may fall under the CCPA definition of “sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising.” You can opt out through the in-app consent settings.

6. Data Retention

  • Firebase Analytics: events are retained for 14 months by default, then automatically deleted by Google
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are retained for 90 days after the last occurrence
  • Advertising data: managed by the advertising networks per their policies

You can request earlier deletion of data associated with your installation by contacting us; we will forward the request to the relevant third-party processors and confirm completion.

7. Security

We rely on the security measures of Google Firebase and Google AdMob, both of which use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. The App itself does not store sensitive personal data on your device.

No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure; while we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Contact Us

Questions, requests, or complaints about this Privacy Policy can be sent to:

Email: termobaby@gmail.com

Please use the subject line “Alien Chain Reaction Privacy” so we can route the request promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in-app and will require renewed consent where required by law. The “Last Updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
